How the checks are graded

These lines are VVV watch’s own choices, not targets set by Venice. Burn cover is the main one: below 25%, burns cancel less than a quarter of new VVV, so supply keeps growing at more than three-quarters of the issuance rate. At 100%, burns cancel all new VVV and supply stops growing.

Credit runs out each day if unused. Across all staked DIEM, holders used 53.5% of their daily credit over the last 7 days (VeniceStats). Using about $0.43 a day per DIEM covers the lost rewards.

Sell the DIEM

$2,088Indicative spot value before fees and price impact · $2,088.17 per DIEM (27 Nov 13:07 UTC)

After 1 year of forgone rewards, the sale leaves $1,931 to buy back 1 DIEM, before fees and price impact.

With no other mints or burns, buying back and burning 1 DIEM would release the full 467.7 sVVV locked by this first mint.

The mint rate rises from 665 to 717 sVVV per DIEM as supply passes Venice’s 40,000 DIEM target. Higher rates follow. Current supply is 37,401 DIEM. Buying DIEM back on the market could cost more than you received from selling it.

DIEM trades at 16.3% of the sVVV locked to mint it. To burn staked DIEM, first complete its unstaking cooldown of 1 day.

Venice’s control Safe sets the emission rate, the share on locked stake and the DIEM curve, and has changed them without a vote. Every change is listed under Rules and control.
